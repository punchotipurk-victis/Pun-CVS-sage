The source rule seeds endocardium from *every point on bb/fasc/mod/purk edges*, including proximal bundle origins, within 3.5 cm at distance/2.6 m/s + 4 ms. It does not constrain that fast seed transfer to its own ventricular side. These rules can produce early muscle activation and bypass a blocked branch. The required geodesic solver permits cell-to-cell travel within ventricular muscle and does not apply the fallback's septal-crossing penalty. These are observations/inferences from the provided contract and measured maps, not a new physiological model.

The first-muscle value corresponds to about 162.13 ms on the normal A-clock, versus the preserved legacy ECG onset at about 177.34 ms. The LBBB right-septal minimum is 20.12 ms and the left-septal minimum remains 14.53 ms; this fails the requested direction, as well as the duration target.

In normal mode, 196 classified mesh vertices have no same-class volume sample within 0.3 cm and 655 are unreachable from the specified seeds. They stay NEVER. Do not bridge blood/fibrous tissue, enlarge the radius or silently use Euclidean times to conceal these gaps. Check the JSON report for final counts.
