## B6

Three consecutive complete conduction picking/performance runs: **0 failures**, with SwiftShader on/off ratios **1.23×, 1.18×, 1.24×**. The subsequent full-suite ratio was **1.14×**. The limit remains **1.3×**. Full B6 output: [T6.2-C-B6.txt](T6.2-C-B6.txt).

Pointer checks now require the exact card (the old AVN/His/LBB neighbour exception was removed). The AV bead owns its visible footprint, the His tube has junction priority, and the LBB fan offers a distinct tap location. Tests pause the clock, fix the beat phase, and sample an exposed point for each part.

Performance fixes: packed vertex inputs/interpolants; fewer longitudinal samples while retaining 12 radial segments (8 on phones); one External core pass; a lighter halo that shares buffers; no transparent double-sided tube passes; cached valve anchors; and an Explore fast path for unselected labels. Only See-through needs the depth-classification pair; Cut retains clipping and `uPull` and has no halo or hidden-wall pass. The timing measurement scrolls both page and iframe back to the viewer and averages interleaved samples to avoid vsync median quantisation.
