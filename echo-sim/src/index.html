Ideal view

Moving the probe. Drag the probe body to slide it over the skin. Drag the cyan tip to tilt and rock: the probe pivots on its contact point, like your hand on the handle. Drag the orange ring to rotate (notch). One hand is enough. Keys: arrows slide, QE rotate, WS tilt, AD rock, F fine, B body view, M landmarks. Touch: the same handles with one finger, or two-finger twist.

Simulation model for learning only — not for diagnosis or patient care.
